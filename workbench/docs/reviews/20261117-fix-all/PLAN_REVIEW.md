## Decision standard

V1 and V2 retain their historical meaning. V3 is a separately versioned contract (`parity-absolute-relative/v3`), not a rewrite of old records. For every finite component, the numerical rule is:

```text
abs(actual - reference) <= atol + rtol * abs(reference)
```

Native is reference for native/adapter forward and score comparisons. Every V3 artifact must be finite, shape- and ID-complete, chunk-complete, and bound to one immutable plan. Canonical identity is exactly 13 fields: `checkpoint`, `configuration`, `environment`, `protocol`, `category`, `cohort_id`, `precision`, `seed`, `query_order_sha256`, `gallery_order_sha256`, `reference_exclusions_sha256`, `query_count`, and `gallery_count`. Five controls remain mandatory before a full claim: native repeat, adapter repeat, matched native↔adapter, native B1↔BN, adapter B1↔BN. Batch sensitivity is a diagnostic unless the published method contract requires invariance; it cannot become an invariance PASS by omission.

Preregistered starting tolerances remain fixed: FP32 forward `atol=1e-5, rtol=1e-4`; batch `atol=1e-4, rtol=1e-3`; score `atol=1e-6`; tie `atol=1e-6`. Source/internal AMP and case controls must be explicit. A failed control requires reporting, diagnosis, and a new preregistered basis; thresholds are never widened after observing failure. Independent source score, rank, metrics, identity, and provenance checks are mandatory; a numerical PASS alone is not retrieval or publication acceptance. Full primary population and bounded diagnostic scopes must be named separately.

The V3 contract/gate has durable implementation evidence: `workbench/docs/reviews/20261112-parity-repair/raw/v3-contract/pytest-n10-identity-bound.txt` is the earlier 85-pass/10-warning contract receipt.
