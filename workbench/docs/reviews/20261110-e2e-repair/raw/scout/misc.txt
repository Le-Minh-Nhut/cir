## Cross-model notes (contract-relevant deltas)

- `sprc` is the only target with **no composition adapter** and no `workbench/replay/sprc.py`; its rows above are native-source only.
- `dcnet` is the only one whose native score is **un-normalized inner product + log-softmax fusion**, so its metric units are fractions, unlike the percent-based family.
- `ptha_mtst` is the only gallery that **physically removes** split images whose `.png` is absent (`data_utils.py:174-177`), so its gallery count can differ from the raw split file.
- `tme`, `csmcir`, `sprc`, `ptha_mtst` all share the BLIP-2/Lavis text join and `1 - sim` ascending rank; only TME guards the reference mask with `if category == 'CIRR'` at `precompute_evaluation.py:89`.
